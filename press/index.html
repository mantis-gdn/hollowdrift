Short Bio

Hollow Drift is an industrial groove metal project from Pawtucket, Rhode Island, built around crushing rhythmic guitars, mechanical percussion, deep sub-bass, glitch textures, radio interference, and a recurring broadcast mythology centered on the phrase “I Am the Signal.” The project blends heavy groove, rap-rock cadence, post-hardcore energy, and cyber-industrial atmosphere into a live-forward sound designed to hit physically, not just sit inside headphones.

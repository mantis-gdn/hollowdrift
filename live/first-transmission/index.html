The First Transmission

August 27, 2026 • The Industrial • Pawtucket, Rhode Island

One artist. One witness. One full performance delivered without retreat. This is the permanent record of the night Hollow Drift left the feed and entered the room.

What Happened In The Room

The room was nearly empty. The signal was not.

On August 27, 2026, Hollow Drift staged its first live broadcast at The Industrial in Pawtucket. The system was loaded in. The banner was raised. The lights turned the room blue, purple, and red. The complete performance was ready whether the room filled or remained silent.

Then one listener walked through the door. He bought admission with two five-dollar bills, got a beer, entered the performance room, and stayed. He briefly stepped away for another drink, returned, and remained through the end of the transmission.

Hollow Drift performed the full show. Not a shortened version. Not a rehearsal. The entire broadcast—songs, movement, Signal Preacher interludes, glitches, low end, and all. At the end, the person in the room said he loved it.

The signal became real because it was transmitted.
